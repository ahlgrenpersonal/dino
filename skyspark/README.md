# SkySpark

A touch-first superhero flying adventure with three large, side-scrolling worlds. Fly into robots, slice them with eye lasers, chain electric sparks, and unleash an expanding electric sphere. All powers are available immediately. Normalized damage is punch/ram 1, Sparks and passive electrical aura 0.8, Big Blast 1, and Laser 5. Small robots have 0.8 health, preserving one-hit electric and blast defeats. No health loss, flight fuel, timers, or death screen.

- **Sunbeam City:** sunset rooftops, gardens, markets and a skybridge, ending with the Iron Colossus.
- **Emerald Plains:** grassy hills, lakes, flowers and windmills, ending with a giant tracked excavator, the Earthmover.
- **Stormpeak Range:** snowy cliffs, pines and crystal beacons, ending with the flying robot Stormwing.

Each world is 9,600 units wide. Robot formations populate all 18 stretches of the route: city has 288 ordinary robots, plains 360 and mountains 540, plus eight boss defenders and the boss in each world. Only nearby stretches are active at a time. Flying right activates more robots independently of previous kills. The boss appears near the far end even when earlier robots were bypassed. Defeating the boss completes the level. Ground robots and drones fire large, slow plasma shots at flying targets. Bosses fire missile volleys; the Colossus and Stormwing also telegraph green lasers before firing at a locked position. Missiles travel at 210 units/second, regular shots at 165–185, and the hero flies at 470–500. Projectiles can be dodged; actual hits flash the shield without damage, knockback or loss of progress. Clearing districts restores their landmarks automatically; there are no restoration-star gates.

Touch: tap the sky to fly, a rooftop to land, or a robot to smash. Sparks jumps through up to sixteen nearby robots in sequence (1-second cooldown). Laser starts at the gold-ringed target and sweeps upward or downward toward the larger nearby group with twice the previous angle (up to about 75 degrees), piercing every robot along its beam (2-second cooldown, five damage per target per cast, the strongest individual attack). Big Blast expands around the hero (2.2-second cooldown, 25% wider radius). All three weapons also clear enemy fire: the expanding blast destroys shots inside its bubble and disrupts crossing green beams; the laser cuts shots and beams along its sweep; lightning clears fire near each jump and can target incoming fire when no robot is nearby. Every enemy damage event requires the enemy to intersect the current playfield viewport, including piercing lasers, delayed chain jumps, expanding blasts, the passive aura and flight collisions. Off-screen enemies cannot lose health or count as defeats. Sparks selects only visible robots and ends a chain if its next robot leaves view. Destroying incoming fire does not increase the robot tally. Keyboard: arrows/WASD, X sparks, L laser, Space blast, M map, Escape pause. Fly freely left and right. There is no mission panel, guide arrow, automatic route or within-level fast travel. The top-bar Map button selects worlds; the bottom buttons are Sparks, Laser and Big Blast; the chooser shows three illustrated world postcards with their bosses. The robot tally counts defeats in the current flight.

Defeating a boss celebrates for three foreground seconds, then automatically opens the world chooser. Three countdown lights show the return; pausing or backgrounding freezes the countdown. All three world postcards stay colorful; a lock marks a world still to unlock, and a trophy/check badge marks only the world just completed. Small captions support a parent helping. Reloading a completed flight opens the chooser directly. Completing a world unlocks the next. Selecting a completed world automatically starts a fresh run. Unfinished worlds resume independently, including all defeated robot IDs, boss damage, per-flight tally and position. Returning to earlier stretches preserves defeated robots while unbeaten robots remain available. Original demo and earlier saves migrate locally without resetting progress or sound preference. Boss health is tripled to 12/18/24 armor points; fractional damage is rounded to tenths and preserved in saves; older partial boss damage is scaled to preserve its fraction. A save previously stranded at the far right can now reveal its boss immediately. Manual restart protects the previous save as a recoverable backup and leaves other worlds intact.

Music unlocks on a touch or key press. Returning from the background presents Pause; Keep flying rebuilds interrupted audio on the user gesture. Unmuting also rebuilds the audio graph.

Serve this folder with a static HTTP server. Open index.html on localhost or HTTPS. Install through Safari's Share > Add to Home Screen on iPhone, or a supported browser's install menu. After the first successful online load and service-worker installation, the game can run offline. Worker scope and caches are isolated from sibling games. refresh.html refreshes only SkySpark's app shell and preserves saved progress.

All scenery, characters, effects and music are drawn or synthesized by the app. No reference photographs, external fonts, analytics, accounts or runtime inference calls are used. Existing lightning icons remain unchanged. Application modules and the service-worker shell use build world104.
